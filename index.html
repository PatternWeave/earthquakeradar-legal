Earthquake Radar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Earthquake Radar is an iPhone app that fingerprints a recording on this device and ranks the closest minutes in the JVIZ Earthquake Weather archive.

What Earthquake Radar collects

Earthquake Radar does not create an account. It does not run ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking. It does not sell personal data.

A file you import stays on this iPhone for the study. Video is discarded. The first 30 seconds of audio are turned into a fingerprint on the device. That fingerprint is compared to a bundled archive index. The recording is not uploaded.

The app may write small values to UserDefaults and read file timestamps on this device so a study can resume. Those values do not leave the phone.

Network

Study itself works offline once a file is on the phone. Network is used only to play one-minute archive clips from jviz.net over HTTPS. Those requests do not include your recording.

Permissions

  • Photo Library — optional. You pick a screen recording or audio file. Deny it and you can still import from Files or study a bundled vault sample.
  • Files — optional. You pick a movie or audio file to study.

Third parties

  • jviz.net — archive clip playback only
  • Apple (App Store, Photos, Files)

Earthquake Radar is not an official Apple or xAI product.
